Graduate Courses

Global Poverty and Economic Development

Teaching Assistant for prof. Lorenzo Casaburi · University of Zurich · Fall 2024, Fall 2025

Graduate development economics covering poverty measurement, financial markets for the poor, private sector development and contracting frictions, migration and market access, conservation and climate change. Methodologically, the course combines theory with modern empirical methods, with a particular emphasis on causal inference and program evaluation.

Undergraduate Courses

Global Policy Analysis: Policy, Development, Growth

Teaching Assistant for prof. Dina Pomeranz · University of Zurich · Spring 2024, Spring 2025

The course introduces students to key topics in global development, including poverty measurement, inequality, population growth, health and education, climate change, taxation, and migration, and equips them with tools for empirical analysis, including randomized controlled trials, difference-in-differences, and regression discontinuity designs.
